Clarity gives clinicians a place to prepare for conversations about intimacy and cancer. It brings together practical resources, thoughtful language, and conversation starters for the moments that are hardest to begin. Context is an iOS meeting scribe that listens through the appointment and drafts the note in whatever medical format you already work in. One helps you find the words, the other writes up what was said. Clarity and Context are built in partnership between Petit and Cancer and Intimacy LLC.
