Clarity gives clinicians a place to prepare for conversations about intimacy and cancer: resources, language, and openings for the hardest moments. Context is an iOS scribe that drafts the note in your medical format. One finds the words, the other writes up what was said. A Petit partnership with Cancer and Intimacy LLC.
