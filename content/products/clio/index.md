Clio is a speech-to-text app for talking to your computer instead of typing it all out. Press a key, say what you mean, and the text lands in whatever app you are already working in. Everything runs on your machine, so your voice and your transcripts never leave your Mac. Clio is open source, and the code is on GitHub for anyone to read, audit, or run themselves.
