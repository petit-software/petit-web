Ruby is a survivorship platform for the specialists who look after people after cancer treatment — physiotherapists, psychologists, nutritionists, navigators and nurses. Structured intake and an AI scribe turn each session into notes without the writing up. From those it builds a personalised support plan the patient can follow and the team can track over time. Scheduling, telemedicine and shared documents sit in the same workspace, so care continues between appointments.
